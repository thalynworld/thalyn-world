What the showcase proves

A world you can inspect. Not just admire.

The real thing, not a render

The showcase is designed to display real glTF world exports. Rotate and inspect a scene to see the result of Thalyn's creation-to-export pipeline, not a promotional illustration.

Move how you like

Use the current viewer's orbit and zoom controls to inspect your own files. The completed World Showcase will make Thalyn's own exported worlds available directly on this page.

Your file never leaves you

When you select a local model, the browser viewer loads it on your device rather than uploading it to the showcase. Hosted world links, when enabled, will fetch the exported scene from its hosted location.

Our aim is simple: build it in Thalyn, export it as glTF, then let somebody else see the actual world.
