The export

Then hand it to the people you made it for.

Thalyn includes a Roblox export path under compatibility testing. How geometry, materials, lighting, terrain and interactions transfer will be documented against tested Roblox Studio builds before release.

An exported world is a starting point for a Roblox experience. Platform-specific materials, weather, collisions, performance and gameplay systems can require additional editing in Roblox Studio.

Exporting is free and unlimited. It's a pipeline step, not a generation. And what lands in Roblox is yours — yours to publish, yours to keep.

A world only you have walked is just a place. Export is how it becomes somewhere.

What carries across

Take the shape of your world into a new project.

Thalyn's Roblox workflow is still being validated. The following are export goals to test, not a guarantee of identical rendering or behaviour across engines.

Terrain

Terrain and geometry transfer are being tested; inspect and adjust the resulting map inside Roblox Studio.

The placed world

Placed geometry and assets may require optimisation or reconfiguration after export.

Light

Re-create or adapt the intended lighting in Roblox Studio as needed.

Atmosphere

Sky, fog and weather can behave differently between engines and may need rebuilding.

Solid ground

Collision and player navigation should be tested on the actual target Roblox experience.

Your look

Materials and visual styles may need to be adjusted to Roblox's rendering capabilities.

How it goes

Three steps, start to published.

Inside Thalyn it's three moves: Say how it should feel. Place what you love. Walk in. Here's how that world reaches your players.

01

Make it yours

Use the included assets, set a mood, create a world and inspect it in first person. Own FBX imports are planned for full release.

02

Take it across

Use the Roblox export path once compatibility testing has been completed, then check and adjust the result in Studio.

03

Open the doors

Import into Roblox Studio, verify geometry, materials, collisions and performance, add the gameplay you want, then publish under Roblox's terms.

You've walked it alone since you built it. Step three is the day somebody else walks it too.
