“Every world I make looks like everyone else's.”

Yours from the first step

Most generators hand you their world. Thalyn builds one around yours.

Drop in your own model — a castle, a cottage, a ruin, a monument — and Thalyn treats it as the reason the world exists. Terrain, sightlines and paths arrange themselves so it sits where it should and stays reachable on foot.

Then walk the finished world, open the style selector and choose a different visual character. Every surface, texture and material transforms at once — in real time, while you're standing in it. Not a filter over the top. The whole world. Your feeling, your centrepiece, your look: nobody else lands on the same place.

The first comment on your next place: “wait — how did you make this?”

The export

Then hand it to the people you made it for.

When it feels right, the whole world goes into Roblox — terrain, assets, lighting and atmosphere intact, with triangle budgets enforced so it runs properly on the devices your players actually use.

Nothing flattens on the way over. The hills you climbed, the light at the hour you chose, the fog resting in the valley — all of it arrives walkable and playable. A starter game pack is included in every export, so there's something to do the moment your friends walk in.

Exporting never costs Awen, the in-app credit that covers world generation. It's a pipeline step, not a generation. And what lands in Roblox is yours — yours to publish, yours to keep.

A world only you have walked is just a place. Export is how it becomes somewhere.
