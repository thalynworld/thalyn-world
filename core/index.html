The toolkit

Say how it should feel. Walk the world that forms around it.

Choose how a place should feel, then shape it with paths, buildings, lights and little corners worth staying in. Thalyn brings the landscape, water, weather and wildlife together around your choices. Explore it in first person, from a garden path to the top floor of a tower.

You don't need modelling or scripting skills. Start with Thalyn's included asset collection; draw fences, add places to sit, build walkable interiors and save scenes to reuse. Bringing your own FBX models is planned for the full release after February 2027 Next Fest.

Build a place you love, then step inside and stay a while.

Make somewhere to call your own

Build it. Open the door. Move in.

Build with the included collection of cottages, towers, trees, gardens and scenery. Thalyn gives buildings walkable floors, stairs and rooms that follow their shape. Open shutters, make windows and doors, and find your favourite view. Your own FBX files are planned for the main release, not the November 2026 demo.

A building isn't just scenery when you can make yourself at home inside it.

One pick, a whole new world

Change your world's entire look in a single pick.

Same world. One click. Every surface re-composes while you stand in it — what it's made of, not what it is. Open the style selector in first person, choose, and every texture and object transforms at once, in real time. Every copy has it from day one.

You'll do it once to see if it's real. You'll keep doing it because the eerie version of your serene valley was, somehow, always there.

Voice creation

Say it out loud.

Answer the creation ritual in your own voice — "serene," "surprise me" — and the world takes you at your word. Typing and picking from a menu do exactly the same thing, so use whichever suits the moment.

VR

Stand in it properly.

Your worlds open in PC VR through OpenXR, tested on Meta Quest 3. Scale is the whole point: a valley you shaped on a monitor reads completely differently when you are standing at the bottom of it, looking up. Everything the desktop does stays exactly where it is — VR is another way in, not a separate edition.

Included day one — in every copy

Complete terrain generation

Terrain with real shape and real logic: rivers that carve their own valleys and flow the way water actually flows; roads and footpaths that link the places that matter, with bridges where they must cross; biomes that meet at natural boundaries; and sightlines composed on purpose, so the world keeps offering you somewhere to look and somewhere to shelter.

Focal points

Pick a building or landmark from the included collection as your centrepiece; paths, terrain and sightlines help make it feel like it belongs. Custom FBX import is planned for the full release.

Full atmosphere

Sky, fog, time of day, and ambient mood — all driven by the feeling you chose, never a list of unrelated presets stitched on afterward.

Generation history

Every world you make is saved with its emotion and settings. Return to anything you have ever created, or grow a fresh variation from the same starting point.

Repeatable worlds

The same choices rebuild the same world. Share your choices and anyone can grow the very same world for themselves.

Roblox export

Roblox export is a planned creative workflow still undergoing compatibility tests. glTF export is central to the November 2026 demo; final results and platform-specific limitations will be documented before release.

Included library of looks

An included library gives you places and objects to begin with. The optional ThalynQuest experience, planned for the main release, rewards gentle worldbuilding puzzles with exclusive cosmetics and story chapters — never with paid shortcuts.

A world only you have walked is just a place. Export is how it becomes somewhere.

Six moods

Choose the feeling. The whole world follows.

A colour grade changes the colour. A mood changes everything — the colour, the light, the weather, the water, what grows and what lives there. Six moods. Same seed, six different places.

Serene

Open, gentle, unhurried. Spaces that invite you to rest.

Joyful

Bright, abundant, generous. Worlds that feel alive and welcoming.

Oppressive

Weight and enclosure. A world that closes around you.

Desolate

Emptied. The feeling of something that was, and is no longer.

Eerie

Wrongness at the edge of perception. The unseen that shouldn't be there.

Chaotic

Overwhelm as terrain. A world that refuses to resolve.

A world that reads like somewhere you've been — not something you assembled.

Formed, not farmed

Come back to a place you remember.

A place you love should be easy to return to. Thalyn uses rules-based, repeatable world generation: the same seed and settings rebuild the same generated landscape. Its environmental-psychology foundations help guide how shelter, light, water and atmosphere work together.

Share a seed as a starting point, then make the world your own with building and decoration. The research explains the design; the joy comes from being there.

Come back in six months. It's still there. Exactly.

Awen

Awen — the current that runs through Thalyn.

Awen is the old word for the breath of poetic inspiration — the song worlds are born from.

World generation uses Awen, Thalyn's replenishing creative energy. It refills daily and is not sold. Exploring, decorating, editing and exporting worlds you've already generated do not use generation credits.

ThalynQuest — optional story, main release only

A quiet story hidden in the worlds you make.

Planned for the main release, not the demo: ThalynQuest is an optional, zero-pressure journey through worldbuilding puzzles. Create worlds from story clues and discover a seven-part, approximately 50,000-word novel chapter by chapter. You can enjoy Thalyn without taking part.

Solving its gentle puzzles unlocks exclusive cosmetic rewards that are not available for purchase, along with chapters of the story. The building tools are not gated behind the quest.

The toolkit is complete with or without it. It's a gift, not a gate.

ThalynQuest is planned for the main release; it will not be in the November 2026 demo.
